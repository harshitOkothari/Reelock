Reelock Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 2026

Reelock ("the app") helps you cut down on short-form video by blocking Instagram Reels and YouTube Shorts or limiting how long you watch them. This policy explains what the app does with your information. The short version: Reelock does not collect, share or sell any personal data.

Information we collect

None. Reelock has no accounts, no analytics, no advertising and no internet permission. It cannot send data off your device.

Information stored on your device

Your settings (limits, sleep schedule, answers you give during setup such as your age and usual scrolling time) and simple usage counters (for example, seconds of Reels watched per day and number of blocks) are stored locally on your phone so the app can work and show your statistics. This data never leaves your device and is deleted when you uninstall the app.

Accessibility Service

Reelock uses Android's Accessibility Service solely to detect whether a Reels or Shorts screen is open in the Instagram and YouTube apps, so it can block it or apply your daily limit. The service is restricted to those two apps. It reads only on-screen element identifiers needed for that detection, in memory, at the moment they are checked. If you turn on the optional home-feed options in Instagram, it can also scroll your feed past Reels and posts from accounts you do not follow, and it can press Back or Home to leave a blocked Reel. Stories are never touched. It does not read or store messages, passwords, personal content or anything typed, does not record the screen, and does not transmit anything. You can turn the service off at any time in Android Settings > Accessibility.

Children

Reelock is not directed to children under 13 and does not knowingly collect information from them.

Third parties

Reelock is not affiliated with, endorsed by or sponsored by Instagram, Meta or YouTube/Google. We share no data with any third party.
